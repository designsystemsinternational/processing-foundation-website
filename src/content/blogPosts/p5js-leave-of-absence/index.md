Hello community,

Due to a family emergency, I have taken the difficult decision of temporarily taking a leave of absence from the project lead position and will be placing the p5.js project on maintenance mode until January. I invite the community to continue the necessary work to sustain the project to the extent possible in the interim.

Since starting as the p5.js project lead two months ago, every experience I’ve had with the project and community at large has been incredibly gratifying. I’ve encountered so much creativity, enthusiasm, and passion in such a short time, working on different projects and meeting folks from the community. Consequently, this was a difficult decision to make but one I felt was necessary to ensure that I have the capacity to continue stewarding the p5 community.

During this period, I will have limited availability via email but I look forward to beginning correspondence again in the new year. I want to thank those in the p5 community that have been profoundly compassionate and supportive of me while I navigate these times. I cannot wait to rejoin you all soon.

In solidarity,

Moira
