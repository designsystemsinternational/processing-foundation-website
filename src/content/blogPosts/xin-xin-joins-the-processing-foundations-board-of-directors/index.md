The next step in this direction, which has been unfolding over this year, is with Xin Xin joining the Board of Directors.

Xin has been an active member of the Processing community since 2018. In addition to serving as a [Fellowship Mentor](https://processingfoundation.org/fellowships/fellowships-2018) and participating in the [p5.js Contributor’s Conference](https://processingfoundation.org/advocacy/p5-js-contributors-conference-2019), they’ve led the first global Processing Community Day and organized [Processing Community Day in LA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3-_WcLaiV0), an inclusive event that celebrates art, code, and diversity. As a p5.js educator and an [Assistant Professor of Interaction and Media Design](https://www.newschool.edu/parsons/faculty/xin-xin/) at the New School, Xin co-teaches [Critical Computation Lecture](https://courses.newschool.edu/courses/PGTE5251) with [Katherine Moriwaki](https://www.newschool.edu/parsons/faculty/Katherine-Moriwaki/) and co-taught [Code, Decolonized](https://medium.com/@ProcessingOrg/code-decolonized-x-powrplnt-symposium-2022-6433cc84e9c3) with [shawné michaelain holloway](http://www.shawnemichaelainholloway.com) and [Dejohn Huffman](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dejohn-huffman-0904631a5).

[Xin Xin](https://xin-xin.info) is an interdisciplinary artist, designer, and organizer currently making socially-engaged software that explores the possibilities of reshaping language and power relations. Through mediating, subverting, and innovating modes of social interaction in the digital space, Xin invites participants to relate to one another and experience togetherness in new and unfamiliar ways.

As an artist and designer, their work has been exhibited internationally at Ars Electronica, DIS, Gene Siskel Film Center, Tiger Strikes Asteroid, and Machine Project. Most recently their project [TogetherNet](https://togethernet.org/) was selected for the [2020 Rapid Response for a Better Digital Future Fellowship](https://www.eyebeam.org/rapidresponse/) at Eyebeam. They were also a [2021 Art of Practice Fellow and Community Leader](https://www.sundance.org/blogs/sundance-institutes-new-interdisciplinary-program-names-grantees-art-of-practice-fellows/) at the Sundance Institute. As an organizer, Xin co-founded [voidLab](http://projects.dma.ucla.edu/voidlab), a LA-based intersectional feminist collective dedicated to women, trans, and queer folks.

Born in Taipei, Taiwan and raised in Massachusetts, United States, Xin identifies as a cross-cultural, non-binary, anarcho-feminist and will probably always have a genre-nonconforming practice.
