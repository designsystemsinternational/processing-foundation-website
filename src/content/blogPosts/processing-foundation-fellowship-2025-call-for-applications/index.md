The [**Processing Foundation Fellowship**](https://processingfoundation.org/fellowships) is back! This year’s theme, *Data Storytelling*, invites artists, coders, and researchers to transform data into interactive, visual, and performative storytelling experiences. Fellows receive **$10,000**, mentorship, and community support.

🌟 **New this year!** The **Extended Realities Fellowship** with [The Unusual Suspects Theater Company](https://theunusualsuspects.org/) and [Street Poets](https://www.instagram.com/streetpoetsinc/?hl=en) in Los Angeles, where a fellow will collaborate with youth artists to create innovative digital theater projects.

We are thrilled to team up with a cohort of multi-talented organizations to provide mentorship to our 2025 fellows:

-   [Arab Image Foundation](https://arabimagefoundation.org/)
-   [Interactive Fiction Technology Foundation](https://iftechfoundation.org/)
-   [MIT Open Documentary Lab](https://opendoclab.mit.edu/)
-   [The Data Garden Collective](https://www.data-garden.co/)
-   [The Unusual Suspects Theater Company](https://theunusualsuspects.org/)

### 📅 **Important Dates:**

🔹 Open Call Announcement: March 18, 2025

🔹 Applications Open: April 1, 2025

🔹 Fellowship Info Sessions: [March 29](https://youtu.be/ebEXcQPf9FM) & [April 10, 2025](https://youtu.be/JYEr76gRbv8)

🔹 Applications Close: May 2, 2025 — Extended to Sunday, May 4th at 11:59PM EST 🚨
