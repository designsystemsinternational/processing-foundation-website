Jubril Olambiwonnu is a London-based software engineer and creative
technologist. For more than a decade he has engineered high-scale platforms
across payments and identity verification. Beyond client work, he explores
culture through code in projects such as The Future Protest, a speculative
cartography of Lagos Lagoon, and Atunda, an AI choreography archive. He is also
the founder of Poser, an AI virtual-fitting startup that enables online shoppers
to buy clothes with confidence.
