[Cy X](https://cyberwitch666.com/) (they/we) is an energy worker, earth tender,
and cyber witch working within the realms of sound, video art, performance,
rituals, and care work. Fusing art and technology with the practice of
witchcraft, they use spells, rituals, and alchemic practices to fundamentally
alter the world around us while merging their ancestral technologies alongside
emerging ones. Cy holds a Master’s Degree from New York University’s Interactive
Telecommunications Program and a Bachelor’s Degree from Colorado College Film
and Media Studies Program. Cy was mentored by Johanna Hedva, Director of
Advocacy for Processing Foundation.
