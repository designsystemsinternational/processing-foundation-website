[Angi Chau](http://www.angichau.com/) is an educator, maker, engineer, and
creative coder. She is constantly thinking about how to design and create
learning spaces that celebrate technology, yet allow everyone, especially those
who have traditionally been underrepresented in fields like engineering and CS,
to feel a sense of belonging. Angi will be mentored by Saber Khan, Processing
Foundation’s Education Community Director.
