elekhlekha อีเหละเขละขละ is a collaborative research-based group consisting of
Thai diaspora Bangkok-born, Brooklyn-based artists, Kengchakaj–เก่งฉกาจ and
Nitcha Tothong–ณิชชา โตทอง. They delve into subversive storytelling by exploring
non-hegemonic sounds and visual archives, historical research, decoding, and
unlearning biases.
