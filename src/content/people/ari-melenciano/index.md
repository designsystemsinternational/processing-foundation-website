[Ari Melenciano](https://www.ariciano.com/) is a Brooklyn-based artist,
designer, creative technologist, and [DJ](https://gvo.fm/). She is currently a
graduate student at NYU’s Interactive Telecommunications program, using her
thesis to build an experimental neo-retro camera company called
[Ojo Oro](https://www.ariciano.com/ojooro). She is also the founder and producer
of [Afrotectopia](https://www.afrotectopia.com/), a new media arts, culture, and
technology festival. Ari will be mentored by Jen Kagan, and advised by
[Daniel Shiffman](http://shiffman.net/).
