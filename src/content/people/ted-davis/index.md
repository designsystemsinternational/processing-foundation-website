[Ted Davis](https://teddavis.org/) is a media artist / designer / educator.
Since 2010 he has taught interaction design and coordinated the UIC/HGK MDes
Basel program at The Basel School of Design, HGK FHNW. His open source projects
(basil.js, XYscope, p5.glitch, P5LIVE) enable programming within Adobe InDesign,
rendering graphics on vector displays, byte level glitching and collaborative
live coding with p5.js. His GitHub is available [here](https://github.com/ffd8).
Ted will be mentored by Saber Khan, the Education Community Director of
Processing Foundation.
