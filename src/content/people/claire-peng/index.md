[Claire Peng](https://claire-peng.vercel.app) (she/her) is a Chinese-Canadian
developer and designer based in London. Formerly a menswear designer (Burberry,
A-COLD-WALL\*, DOM REBEL), her designs have been worn by artists like Chance the
Rapper and Machine Gun Kelly. Now working as a software engineer, she specialises
in UX/UI design, web accessibility, and advocates for making tech approachable
and inclusive to those from non-traditional backgrounds. Outside of work, she is
exploring creative and live coding, makes clothes, and sings in the
shoegaze-inspired band, Sunnbrella.
