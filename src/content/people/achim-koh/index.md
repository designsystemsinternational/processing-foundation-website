[Achim Koh](https://scalarvectortensor.net/) is a programmer, translator, and
educator, currently based in Seoul, who works around the techno-politics of
machine learning, digital culture, and knowledge production. He is interested in
technological literacy that enables social progress and democratization rather
than simply reproducing existing power structures. Achim was mentored by
[Joey Lee](https://jk-lee.com/).
