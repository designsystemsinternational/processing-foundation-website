[Gottfried Haider](http://ghai.xyz/) is an artist, educator and tool-maker. His
background is Digital Arts, with a degree from the University of Applied Arts
Vienna. He is also recipient of a Fulbright Scholarship and holds an MFA in
Design Media Arts from the University of California Los Angeles (UCLA).
Gottfried was mentored by [Ben Fry](http://benfry.com). Read about his
fellowship project
[here](http://medium.com/@ProcessingOrg/thoughts-on-processing-on-arm-1e2f82c36d38).
