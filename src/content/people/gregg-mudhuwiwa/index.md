Gregg Tendai Mudhuwiwia is control systems engineer and entrepreneur based in
Malaysia. His interest areas include STEM coordination, speculative engineering
design and technology education. He is the co-founder of Orion STEAM Labs, a
Shenzhen-based STEAM education design company, that works both with schools and
training institutions in Asia to integrate coding, robotics and design programs
into their practice. He was recently a researcher at The Terraforming urban
design research program at Strelka Institute in Moscow and continues to research
questions around the Anthropocene and its impacts on the future of work.
[https://github.com/gnicezw](https://github.com/gnicezw) Gregg will be mentored
by Shawn Patrick Higgins.
