[Tommy Martinez](https://thomasjohnmartinez.com/) (he/him) is an artist and programmer working through research, sound and code. He is working on [p5.sound.js](https://github.com/processing/p5.sound.js), and is the author of [p5.spatial.js](https://github.com/ogbabydiesal/p5.spatial.js), a p5-addon library that addresses multichannel sound systems.
