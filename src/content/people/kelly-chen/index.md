Kelly Chen is an interdisciplinary researcher working and living in Boston, Massachusetts. In her art practice, Chen works with hardware and software to reveal the intersecting histories of computation, craft, moving image, and cartography as they relate to authority, labor, and resistance in social, political, and economic systems. Her performances utilize amplified surfaces, motors, sensors, field recordings, and live data. Her work is available at [luckystarbus.cargo.site](https://luckystarbus.cargo.site)
