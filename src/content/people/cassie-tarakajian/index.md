[Cassie Tarakajian](https://github.com/catarak) is a software developer,
hardware engineer, creative technologist, and artist. She is a cofounder at the
digital creative agency [Girlfriends](http://girlfriends.site), an engineer at
Cycling '74, and a contributor to open source. She is interested in ways that
art drives technology and vice versa. Cassie was mentored by
[Daniel Shiffman](http://shiffman.net) and
[Lauren McCarthy](http://lauren-mccarthy.com). **This Fellowship is sponsored by
[NYU ITP](http://itp.nyu.edu).** Read about her fellowship project
[here](http://medium.com/@ProcessingOrg/a-p5-js-web-editor-for-all-64aaa3f9d767).
