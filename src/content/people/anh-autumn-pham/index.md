[Anh (Autumn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/anh-hathu-pham/) Pham) is a Deaf Queer
tech worker and writer from Vietnam, based in Vancouver, BC. By day, they work
at a financial tech company. They also write “Letters from Anh” newsletter on
accessible tech, disability, and humanity (in English and Vietnamese). Anh helps
organize mentorship programs for Reboot and Viet Tech communities, makes decent
matcha, and loves hosting dinners and board game nights. Anh cares a lot about
accessibility and community care, and is broadly curious about people,
histories, cultures, policies, and the intersection of tech and art and society.
