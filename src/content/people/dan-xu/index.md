[Dan Xu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/danxu-332346143/) (CN/NL) is a creative
researcher and technologist in the fields of human-computer interaction and
interactive art. Currently, she is pursuing her doctoral degree at Leiden
University. Her research focuses on exploring new ways to conceptualize
interaction, aiming to enhance understanding of the dynamic exchange between
interacting elements and stimulate the creation of new interactive dialogues.
Besides research, she enjoys creating playful interactive prototypes and
experiences with code, sound, and text.
