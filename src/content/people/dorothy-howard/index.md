[Dorothy Howard](http://dorothyhoward.com/) is a researcher and open technology
advocate from Mount Vernon, Washington. Dorothy has organized around equity and
human rights in Wikipedia and was Wikimedian-in-Residence at the Metropolitan
New York Library Council, where she facilitated public knowledge and
instructional technology projects. She has spoken at The Society for the Social
Studies of Science, Scientific Computing with Python, and in other venues and
classrooms. Her research appears in Computer-Human Interaction and
Computer-Supported Cooperative Work. Dorothy has an MA in Communication from the
University of California San Diego, and a BA in History from Reed College. She
lives in Brooklyn, NY.
