[Yash Soni](https://github.com/skyash-dev) (he/him) is a Computer Science

digital tools, like Blender and game engines. Over time, he explored game
development, web development, design, and animation, eventually developing a

the p5.js community, where he is interested in improving accessibility and
debugging experiences for beginners learning shaders.
