[Ahnjili Zhuparris, PhD](https://www.artificialnouveau.com) (NL, USA), is an AI
engineer and artist working between New York and the Netherlands. She develops
AI applications for the medical and cosmetic industries and researches
biomarkers for mental and physical well-being using machine learning. Her
artistic and communication efforts focus on raising awareness about AI and
algorithmic violence. Ahnjili holds a PhD in Clinical Neuropharmacology, a
Master’s in Cognitive Neuroscience, and a Bachelor's in Neuroscience. A
recipient of the Mozilla Creative Media Award, her work has been supported by
the Mozilla Foundation, IMPAKT, and Constant, and showcased at Ars Electronica,
Articulating Data, and CICA Museum.
