Zahra Hassan (they/them) is an interdisciplinary researcher, writer, activist,
and educator from Vienna with roots in Iraq. ​​With a background in journalism,
media, and anti-discrimination advocacy, they are dedicated to bridging
disciplines with an emphasis on the intersection of environmental studies and
creative practice. Throughout their Master’s at NYU’s Experimental Humanities
and Social Engagement program, their work has been revolving around Iraqi orange
trees and what it means to tap into their senses when conducting research. They
are currently finishing a piece of speculative fiction about environmental
disaster to contribute to public facing scholarship. Their work is available at
[https://www.sarahassan.at](https://www.sarahassan.at)
