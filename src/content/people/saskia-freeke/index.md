[Saskia Freeke](http://www.sasj.nl) is an artist, creative coder, interaction
and visual designer. She is interested in creating playful experiences. She
makes daily art, mainly generated with code, since January 1, 2015. She is a
member of Code Liberation and is doing her masters in Computational Arts at
Goldsmiths University of London. Saskia was mentored by
[Phoenix Perry](http://phoenixperry.com) and
[Johanna Hedva](http://johannahedva.com). Read more about her fellowship project
[here](http://medium.com/@ProcessingOrg/community-and-code-882b00e6ee32).
