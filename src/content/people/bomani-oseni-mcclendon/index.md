[Bomani Oseni McClendon](https://bomani.xyz/) is an engineer living in Brooklyn.
Bomani builds software to support artists, and teaches students grades 2–5 about
electronic circuitry through craft activities. Through his creative practice, he
studies the ways that Black health outcomes are influenced by a history of
scientific racism. Bomani’s Github is [here](https://github.com/bomanimc/).
Bomani will be mentored by [Joey Lee](https://jk-lee.com/).
