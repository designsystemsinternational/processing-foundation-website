I am a Cannes Innovation Lions and Ennies awards' losing maker of things residing in Guelph, ON, Canada. Usually those things require code, rarely but sometimes power tools and hand tools, most of the time it's for other people, and often it's done with others. I specialise in radical generalism, have a keen focus on ethics in technology, and am more concerned with why we make the thing and the people we are making for over how we make it.
