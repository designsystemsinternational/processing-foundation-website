Akshat Patil is a 19 year old computer science undergraduate from Indore, India,
who enjoys building where graphics, creative coding, and open source meet. He
began contributing to p5.js in December 2025, working on p5.strands, WebGL,
WebGPU, testing infrastructure, and rendering features. He is particularly
interested in making powerful graphics tools more accessible and exploring the
future of creative coding on the web.
