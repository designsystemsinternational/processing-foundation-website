[Shakeel Mohamed](https://shakeel.design/) (he/him) is a Brand Designer in Los
Angeles with an MFA in Graphic Design from ArtCenter College of Design. During
his software engineering career he worked on open source developer tools, high
performance backend systems, and a data simulation engine. He found Processing
and p5.js while applying to design school in 2021. Typography is the core of his
practice, from writing code to designing typefaces.
