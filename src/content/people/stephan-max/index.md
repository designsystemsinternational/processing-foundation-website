[Stephan Max](https://stephanmax.com/) (he/him) is a freelance software developer
based in Cologne, Germany. With a focus on working with NGOs/NPOs and civil
society associations, he aims at teaching, producing, and maintaining tech for
those who don’t have natural access to it. He can offer expertise in developing
web platforms, data analysis tooling, and playful creative experiences. Stephan
is an avid moviegoer, takes regular field trips to museums and exhibitions, and
lately dabbles in making art with his trusty pen plotter.
