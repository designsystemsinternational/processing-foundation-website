[Kenneth Lim](https://designerken.be/designing) is an interaction designer from
Malaysia. He is interested in language, culture, and human/machine interactions.
His work often touches on the nonsensical and looks into how things can or
cannot be translated between languages and mediums. He likes to play video games
but often can’t find the time. Kenneth will be mentored by
[Xin-Xin](https://xin-xin.info/), and advised by
[Lauren McCarthy](http://lauren-mccarthy.com/).
