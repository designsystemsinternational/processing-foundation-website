[Abdellah Iraamane](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aairaamane/) is a Moroccan
software engineer and social activist based in Dublin, Ireland. He has been
working on youth development projects aiming to enhance and build the capacities
of rural and suburban youth, alongside a full-time career as a developer. He
likes clean code and pizza, and strives to achieve universal access to quality
education. Abdellah was mentored by
[George Boateng](https://www.linkedin.com/in/georgegboateng/), who was a Fellow
in 2018, and served as a mentor for Prince Steven Annor, a 2019 Fellow.
