[Andreas Refsgaard](https://andreasrefsgaard.dk/) is an artist and creative
coder based in Copenhagen. He uses algorithms and machine learning to allow
people to play music using only eye movement and facial gestures, control games
by making silly sounds or transform drawings of musical instruments on paper
into real compositions. Andreas was mentored by [Yining Shi](https://1023.io/).
