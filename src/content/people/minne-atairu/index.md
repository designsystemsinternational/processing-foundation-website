Minne Atairu is an interdisciplinary Artist, and doctoral student at Columbia
University. Minne’s academic research emerges at the intersection of Machine
Learning, Art Education and Hip-Hop Pedagogy. Through the use of Artificial
Intelligence (StyleGAN, GPT-3), Minne recombines historical fragments,
sculptures, texts, images and sounds to generate synthetic Benin Bronzes which
often hinge on questions of repatriation, and post-repatriation.
Minneatairu.com. Minne will be mentored by Layla Quinones.
