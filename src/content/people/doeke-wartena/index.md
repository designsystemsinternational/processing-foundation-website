[Doeke Wartena](http://www.doekewartena.nl) is a graphic and interaction
designer working across the fields of creative code, typography, and data
visualization. Besides that he runs a hard software lab at ArtEZ University of
the Arts, in Arnhem, the Netherlands. When it comes to software, both
performance as well as an easy-to-use API, are important goals for him. Doeke
will be mentored by Casey Reas.
