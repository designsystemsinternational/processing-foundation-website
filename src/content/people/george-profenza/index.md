[George Profenza](http://sensori.al/) is a London-based, Romanian-born creative
technologist, most-of-the-time socially functioning nerd, all-around fix-it code
champion, and chaser of rabbit holes. He spends his spare time bringing robots
and puppets to life and finding artistic inspiration in paper folding, doodling,
and complex geometry. [His GitHub is here](https://github.com/orgicus/). George
was mentored by Golan Levin.
