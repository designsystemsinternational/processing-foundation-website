[Kalila Shapiro](https://www.kalilashapiro.com/) is a researcher, creative
technologist, and human-computer interaction designer. She is writing a series
of pieces on the ethics of virtual reality for All Tech Is Human, is the Social
Media Lead for MedVR, and is the deputy leader of the Application Accessibility
group for XR Access. [Her GitHub is here](https://github.com/kalilashapiro).
Kalila was mentored by [Luis Morales-Navarro](https://lm-n.com/), who was a 2018
Fellow, and [Claire Kearney-Volpe](https://www.clairekv.com/), a 2016 Fellow and
a current member of Processing Foundation’s Board of Advisors.
