[Saber Khan](https://medium.com/@ed_saber) is a veteran K12 educator with
fifteen years of teaching experience in public, private, and charter schools. He
organizes the Creative Coding Festival, [CC Fest](https://ccfest.rocks/), a free
and inclusive creative coding event for K12 students and teachers. And he leads
the #ethicalCS project, a collaborative project to build ethically minded
Computer Science educational resources. Saber will be mentored by
[Daniel Shiffman](http://shiffman.net/).
