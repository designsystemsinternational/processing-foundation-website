[Dora Do](http://www.doradocodes.com) is a Creative Technologist based in
Brooklyn, NY, exploring the intersection of technology and art. With extensive
experience in building large-scale tech solutions, combined with her passion for
analog art mediums, Dora aims to understand and deepen the human connection with
technology by creating thoughtful and immersive digital experiences. You can
view her work at www.doradocodes.com.
