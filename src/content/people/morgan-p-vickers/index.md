Morgan P. Vickers is a writer, researcher, community historian, historic
preservation storyteller, ethnographer, and Ph.D. candidate in the Department of
Geography at the University of California, Berkeley. Their present work focuses
on drowned towns of the Santee-Cooper Project in South Carolina, wherein 901
mostly Black families were displaced in the name of New Deal “progress.”
Thematically, Morgan’s work contemplates Black geographies and placemaking,
federal dam and reservoir projects, affect, community memory studies, and
questions of belonging. website: morganpvickers.com. Morgan will be mentored by
Adrian Jones in the summer and Anna Garbier from SOSO, one of our fellowship
sponsors in the fall.
