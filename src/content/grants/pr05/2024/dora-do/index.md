title: Prototype a Collaborative Desktop Editor for Processing
grantees:
  - Dora Do
---

Develop an evolutionary prototype of a desktop editor for Processing that supports collaborative editing, aimed at teaching and facilitating creative coding, focusing on simplicity and accessibility for beginners. This project required development from the ground up, presenting a unique opportunity to contribute to a tool that could significantly influence how programming is taught in creative and educational contexts.
