The p5.js Friendly Error System (FES) enhances the development and debugging experience in p5.js with simplified error messages. With the p5.js 2.0 release, significant updates were required to align FES with the new system architecture. This project made a meaningful impact on a critical component of p5.js, which is particularly beneficial for new coders and enhances the experience for millions of users.
