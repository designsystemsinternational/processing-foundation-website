This project focuses on initiating the migration of the p5.js editor to TypeScript. The goal is to improve maintainability and the developer experience by enhancing readability, enforcing consistency, and reducing the likelihood of errors. The migration process involves selecting portions of the editor, converting them to TypeScript, and ensuring compatibility with the existing codebase. Rather than a full migration, this is an initial step to provide a reference for future contributors as they continue this work. Updating tests and documentation is also important to onboard future contributors.
