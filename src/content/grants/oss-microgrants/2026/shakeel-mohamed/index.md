### **Research and Benchmark p5.js Typography Performance**

Develop performance tests for p5.js typography functions across the 2D, WebGL, and WebGPU renderers. Beginning with textToPoints(), the research examines how factors such as text length, font size, sampling, and rendering affect performance, while documenting methods for comparing p5.js 1.x and 2.x. The resulting benchmark framework also creates a foundation for evaluating textToContours(), textToPaths(), and textToModel() and identifying opportunities for future optimization. [View the work in progress.](https://github.com/processing/p5.js/pull/8918)
