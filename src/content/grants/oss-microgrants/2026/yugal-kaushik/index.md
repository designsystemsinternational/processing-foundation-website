### **Improve p5.js Editor Reliability and Stewardship**

Strengthen the Editor’s asset-management system through targeted bug fixes and ongoing maintenance. The work enforces storage limits with clear user-facing messages, prevents uploads from becoming permanently orphaned when sketches are not saved, and adds a cleanup process for abandoned files. Additional investigations into missing assets and broken file references help ensure that users can reliably view, manage, and delete their uploaded materials while supporting the Editor’s long-term maintainability. [View the orphaned-assets contribution.](https://github.com/processing/p5.js-web-editor/pull/4119)
