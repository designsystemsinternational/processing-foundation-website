### **Create a p5.js 2.0 Transition Guide for Educators**

Develop an accessible guide that helps middle and high school educators update their existing curricula and classroom projects from p5.js 1.x to 2.0. Drawing on a review of widely used creative coding curricula, the guide focuses on the changes most likely to affect classroom instruction, including input events and asset loading. Clear code comparisons and compatibility options help educators transition to 2.0 while preserving the learning content of older lessons. [View the tutorial.](https://p5js.org/tutorials/v2_transition/)
