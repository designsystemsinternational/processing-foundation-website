### **Improve WCAG Accessibility in the p5.js Web Editor**

Strengthen the Editor’s light, dark, and high-contrast themes through clearer link styling, improved color contrast, and visual cues that do not rely on color alone. Develop a color-palette contrast checker and update contributor guidance so accessibility is considered throughout future development. This project makes the Editor easier to navigate and understand for users with low vision and color-vision differences while supporting more consistent, accessible design practices.
