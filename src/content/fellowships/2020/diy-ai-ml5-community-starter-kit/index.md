DIY AI: ML5 Community Starter Kit is a toolkit that teaches beginners how to set up and train an artificial intelligence using ML5. The kit will include instructions for how to find, clean, and process data to feed into a machine-learning algorithm, along with a step-by-step guide for running small in-person workshops. Emily is one of four ml5.js Fellows for 2020, a partnership between Processing Foundation and NYU’s [Interactive Telecommunications Program](https://tisch.nyu.edu/itp) that specifically focuses on [ml5.js](https://ml5js.org/). Read an interview with Emily about her project [here](https://medium.com/processing-foundation/what-can-machine-learning-teach-us-about-ourselves-65b268431890).
