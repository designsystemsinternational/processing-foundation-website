Angi’s project, “Starter Kit for Teaching Creative Coding with p5.js,” asks: How might we empower and support new teachers, who are themselves learning CS, to teach creative coding? I want to investigate the space between pre-made, “plug and play” curricula and a total blank canvas, and create a starter kit of adaptable modules for building one’s own creative coding curriculum. Read an interview with Angi about her project [here](https://medium.com/processing-foundation/three-layer-cs-cake-how-to-make-sure-every-kid-gets-some-cake-f7ee52e147f1).
