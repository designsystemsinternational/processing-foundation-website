Pê Cinco: Internationalization and Popularization for Portuguese Speakers: This project comes from the desire to make p5.js accessible to the Portuguese-speaking community by translating the p5.js reference. Along with the translation we produced a series of short videos about p5.js, and directly engage with the community in Brazil. The project is documented on Instagram [here](https://www.instagram.com/p5jsbrasil/). You can read an interview with the Fellows about their work [here](https://medium.com/processing-foundation/translating-p5-js-into-portuguese-for-the-brazilian-community-14b969e77ab1).
