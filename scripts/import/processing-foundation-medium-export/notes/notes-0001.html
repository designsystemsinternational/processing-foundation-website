  • 2019-07-17 1:12 pm — on Interview with 2019 Fellow Prince Steven Annor
    PSA: The idea for SuaCode was birthed out of the challenge of low computer ownership among students that was encountered during Nsesa Foundation’s annual innovation boot camp, Project iSWEST 2017. I was Lead Facilitator for the Computer Programming course working with George, cofounder and president of Nsesa Foundation. From our survey, George and I realized that 100% of our students had smartphones whereas only 25% had personal laptops. Recognizing an opportunity to innovate, we modified our Programming curriculum which was based on Processing so that teaching and learning could be done using smartphones, circumventing the problem of inadequate laptops.
