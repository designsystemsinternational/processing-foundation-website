📢 Processing Foundation Fellowship 2025: Call for Applications

🚨 DEADLINE EXTENDED: Sunday, May 4th at 11:59PM EST 🚨

The Processing Foundation Fellowship is back! This year’s theme, Data Storytelling, invites artists, coders, and researchers to transform data into interactive, visual, and performative storytelling experiences. Fellows receive $10,000, mentorship, and community support.

🌟 New this year! The Extended Realities Fellowship with The Unusual Suspects Theater Company and Street Poets in Los Angeles, where a fellow will collaborate with youth artists to create innovative digital theater projects.

We are thrilled to team up with a cohort of multi-talented organizations to provide mentorship to our 2025 fellows:

  • Arab Image Foundation
  • Interactive Fiction Technology Foundation
  • MIT Open Documentary Lab
  • The Data Garden Collective
  • The Unusual Suspects Theater Company

📅 Important Dates:

🔹 Open Call Announcement: March 18, 2025

🔹 Applications Open: April 1, 2025

🔹 Fellowship Info Sessions: March 29 & April 10, 2025

🔹 Applications Close: May 2, 2025 — Extended to Sunday, May 4th at 11:59PM EST 🚨
