2021 Fellowships Open Call Moved to Next Year

Hi everyone,

We’d like to announce that the Processing Foundation is shifting the timeline of our 2021 Fellowship Program. The Open Call application period, which usually happens in November, will now open in spring of 2021; and the Fellowships themselves will take place over summer 2021. You can follow us here or on Twitter, or sign up for our mailing list, to be alerted when the application period opens.

After a strange and difficult year, we decided it would be best to give ourselves and our community a little more time to adjust to the new COVID-19 reality, in the hope that we can better support our next cohort of Fellows. We plan to adapt the 2021 Fellowship Program to better respond to concerns of social distancing, remote gatherings, and accessibility on the web.

Since 2013, the Fellowship Program has been a way for us to support members of our community with financial and practical resources, mentorship and guidance, and connections with other kindreds in the community. We are excited to think about how we can continue to foster these in a changed and changing society.

Onward!

—Johanna Hedva, Director of Advocacy & Fellowship Program Lead
