The Processing Foundation is hiring a part-time Grants & Finance Manager!

The Processing Foundation’s mission is to promote software literacy within the visual arts, and visual literacy within technology-related fields — and to make these fields accessible to diverse communities. The Grants & Finance Manager will play a key role in the grant writing, grant management, fundraising, and financial management of our organization.

The Grants & Finance Manager will work closely with our Executive Director, and will collaborate with the rest of the Processing Foundation team. We are an organization that values learning, and aims to create opportunities for learning within the position.

Responsibilities will include:

Grants Management & Fundraising (50%)

  • Research, plan, and write grants.
  • Ensure complete documentation of funding awards.
  • Work with Processing Foundation team to comply with funder’s terms and conditions, manage deliverables, monitor spending, request amendments, and plan spend-out.
  • Manage community donation program, building out a donor base.
  • Assist in running annual fundraiser.

Financial Management (50%)

  • Analyze and monitor foundation’s accounts and cash balances.
  • Ensure timely and accurate processing of payables, deposits, and billings.
  • Support budget creation, budget projections and analysis of expenses as needed.
  • Prepare quarterly financial reports. Document and address issues requiring attention and/or further discussion.
  • Work with accountant to prepare annual taxes and non-profit paperwork.

This is an hourly position targeting an average of 10 hours per week, at a rate of $30–35/hr, depending on experience. The position does not include benefits. This is a one year contract position with a possibility to extend. Our team has members based in New York, Los Angeles, San Francisco, and Berlin, and we work together online, and in person when we can. The Grants & Finance Manager will work primarily remotely, and can be based anywhere.

Desired qualifications include:

  • BA/BS degree in business, nonprofit financial management or accounting (preferred).
  • Two or more years of experience managing grants and finances.
  • Budgeting experience.
  • Proficiency with accounting software, spreadsheets, and data management systems.
  • Excellent communication skills.
  • Ability to pay close and accurate attention to details.
  • Commitment to social justice, diversity, and inclusion, in alignment with the Processing Foundation’s mission.

The Processing Foundation provides equal employment opportunities to all without regard to race, ethnicity, color, religion, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, age, disability, or genetics. Candidates from groups generally underrepresented in the arts or tech fields are especially encouraged to apply.
