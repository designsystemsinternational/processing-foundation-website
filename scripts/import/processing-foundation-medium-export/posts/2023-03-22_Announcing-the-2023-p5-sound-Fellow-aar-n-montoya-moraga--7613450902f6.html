Announcing the 2023 p5.sound Fellow aarón montoya-moraga!

We’re thrilled to announce aarón montoya-moraga as the 2023 p5.sound Fellow!

The p5.sound Fellow will focus on re-envisioning sound in the p5.js project, as a beginner-friendly intro to working with sound on the web and integrating sound into interactive audiovisual projects. They will review existing features and redefine a lean set of core features for p5.sound (like playing sound, handling mic input, and some basic analysis of sound), while leaving more advanced features, like music composition, to other external libraries. The fellow will also develop a robust plan and documentation for future maintenance and contributors. Aarón will be mentored by Kristin Galvin, with support from advisors Jason Sigal, Luisa Pereira, and Yotam Mann and the p5.js leads (p5.js lead, Qianqian Ye and p5.js editor lead, Rachel Lim), to help re-envision the future of sound in p5.js.

The p5.Sound Fellow is a remote position and will be expected to commit 200 hours of work over the course of February to July of 2023. For the duration of the fellowship, the key responsibilities will entail: envisioning the roadmap, writing documentation, and developing a core set of features for working with sound in p5.js.

aarón will develop the p5.sound library with an updated roadmap, foster community contribution, and plan its potential integration into the p5.js core library, with a focus on building creative coding communities in Latin America and creating experimental and non-Western sound examples.

Stay tuned for all the amazing work they will be doing in the coming months!

p5.Sound Fellow: aarón montoya-moraga (they/them) is a Chilean media artist and educator, who writes tutorials, classes and software libraries for arts, with a strong focus on promoting diversity and digital rights. Aarón is a visiting professor at Universidad de Chile’s school of design, and a contributor to Processing Foundation’s p5.js project since 2015, and currently is the 2023 p5.js sound fellow.

p5.Sound Fellow Mentor: Kristin Galvin (she/her) is a senior software engineer at Nvidia currently working with TypeScript and React on serverless architectures. She enjoys making generative synthesizers in the browser and playing music as Kevin Blechdom (and Blectum from Blechdom). She received her doctorate in music composition and performance from CalArts in 2013, and her BA and MFA in electronic music from Mills College. She is looking forward to helping with a new iteration of sound tools for p5.js.
